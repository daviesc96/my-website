## Concept

The idea is to create an instrument where you navigate a 2D space, and your position determines both which notes play and how they're voiced. It's less about playing melodies and more about exploring harmonic territories—understanding music as a landscape.

<Gallery photos={["device_4/analog-03.jpg", "device_4/analog-04.jpg"]} />

## Design Elements

- **2D touchpad input** (X/Y coordinates map to pitch/voicing)
- **Harmonic constraint system** to keep notes musically coherent
- **Velocity sensitivity** for dynamics
- **Visual map** showing harmonic relationships as a grid
- **Pre-defined harmonic spaces** (diatonic, pentatonic, chromatic modes, etc.)

## Core Features

- 8 simultaneous note voices with independent envelopes
- Real-time voicing changes based on harmonic rules
- Continuous pitch expression via gesture
- Selectable harmonic maps (scales, chords, modal territories)
- MIDI and CV output for external sequencers

## The Story Behind It

Wanted to create a more intuitive way to think about chord changes. Instead of thinking "what note comes next," think "what harmonic color am I moving toward?" The 2D space represents that continuous harmonic space.

## Building Process

1. Started with simple scale mapping to XY coordinates
2. Added voice leading rules (smooth voice movement)
3. Implemented chord-space maps (specific voicings at different positions)
4. Added visual feedback showing harmonic relationships
5. Integrated probability systems for subtle variations

## Musical Results

Works best for ambient, evolving pieces where you're exploring rather than playing predetermined sequences. The limitation of the harmonic constraints paradoxically feels more *expressive* because the instrument guides you toward musically coherent choices.

## Unique Aspect

The visual display of the harmonic map made me understand voice leading intuitively for the first time. Seeing how notes should move to stay efficient and smooth was more instructive than years of theory study.
